Booking & travel policies

Reservations

Bookings are subject to availability and confirmed upon receipt of confirmation from Precise Transfers. Please provide accurate pickup, flight, passenger and luggage details.

Payment

Payment requirements and any deposit will be communicated before booking confirmation.

Cancellations

At least 24 hours’ notice is required. Any deposit paid will be retained for cancellations within 24 hours of pickup or no-shows.

Airport Arrivals

Your chauffeur will wait until you complete immigration, customs and baggage collection, with no fixed waiting limit or waiting fees. Please notify us of flight changes.

Other Transfers

Includes 15 minutes of complimentary waiting. After that, $50 applies for every additional 15-minute period or part thereof. Extended waiting is subject to availability.

Changes & Extra Stops

Booking changes are subject to availability. Additional stops or services may affect your quoted price.

Vehicle Conduct

No smoking, eating or drinking inside vehicles. Seat belts must be worn, and respectful conduct is expected.

Children

Please request any required car seats in advance so availability can be confirmed.
