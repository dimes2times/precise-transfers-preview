## Recommended direction — proposed, not a design

Positioning: personal, dependable premium transportation supported by a clear process and authentic local imagery. Likely audiences include visitors, hotel/villa guests, families and small groups; confirm the actual customer mix.

Use the existing black, blue and silver identity as the starting point. Consider dark feature areas, restrained blue accents, neutral silver details and light backgrounds for longer text and forms. Preserve a polished feel without extending metallic effects into every element.

Explore an elegant headline typeface paired with a highly readable body typeface after branding approval. Exact fonts, colors and spacing remain undecided. Use generous spacing, strong text contrast, clear headings and real vehicle/location photography. Avoid generic limousine imagery and unsupported luxury claims.

On mobile, prioritize readable rates, easy service selection, large touch targets and a short step-by-step booking form. Use a persistent booking action only if it does not cover content or keyboard controls. Keep contact methods easy to find.

Use “Request a Transfer” if availability requires manual approval. Use “Book Your Transfer” only when the process genuinely supports confirmed bookings. Use “Request a Package Quote” for custom arrangements.

## Booking and payment proposal

Recommended initial flow if availability is managed manually:

1. Visitor chooses a service and supplies trip details.
2. Visitor sees the applicable rate or an explicit quote-needed message, including confirmed inclusions and charges.
3. Company checks availability and confirms the final quote.
4. Customer pays through secure online checkout or follows the approved alternative payment process.
5. Customer receives a booking reference, payment status, trip details and contact instructions.

Instant booking is an alternative only after vehicle availability, scheduling conflicts, route pricing and payment/confirmation rules can be enforced. A submitted form must not imply a confirmed booking, and a payment must not be mistaken for guaranteed availability.

Suggested form fields:

- Service type and one-way/round-trip selection.
- Pickup/drop-off locations, travel date and local pickup time.
- Arrival airline/flight number where relevant.
- Departure flight time where relevant, distinct from pickup time.
- Separate return-leg details for round trips.
- Passenger count and luggage quantities.
- Child-seat requirements and other relevant assistance requests, subject to confirmed capabilities.
- Lead passenger name, email and telephone with country code.
- Optional notes.
- Clear trip/price summary and acknowledgement of applicable booking policies.

Avoid collecting passport details or raw payment-card details in the general booking form. A suitable payment provider should handle card entry. Select a provider only after verifying eligibility for the client's business location, settlement account, currency and intended integration. Do not assume a particular provider is available.

Decisions needed: full payment versus deposit; payment before or after acceptance; balance collection; refunds and cancellation handling; cash booking rules; how Zelle payments are matched and verified; receipts; notifications; booking administrator; and response expectations. Never mark an offline payment received solely because a visitor selects that method or uploads a screenshot.
